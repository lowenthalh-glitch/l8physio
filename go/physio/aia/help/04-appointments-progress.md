# Appointments, Progress Logs, Session Reports and Home Feedback

## Create an appointment
1. Sidebar: Appointments, then Add.
2. "Appointment Details" tab: Client *, Therapist *, Start Time * and End Time * are required. Optionally pick the Treatment Plan, set the Status (for example Scheduled or Confirmed) and a Location.
3. "Notes" tab: Therapist Notes and Client Notes.
4. Click Save.
To change an appointment later, click Edit on its row.

## Log client progress
1. Sidebar: Progress Logs, then Add.
2. Fill in Client * and Log Date * (required). Optionally pick the Treatment Plan and the Appointment, enter the Overall Pain Level (0-10) and add General Notes.
3. Click Save.

## Session reports and home feedback
- Session Reports (sidebar) lists the reports of completed sessions. A client's reports are also in the Session Reports tab of the client's details popup (Clients, then click the client's row).
- Home Feedback (sidebar) lists feedback clients reported about their home exercises. A client's feedback is also in the Home Feedback tab of the client's details popup.
