# Clients and Therapists

## Create a client
1. In the sidebar click Clients.
2. Click the Add button above the table. The "Physio Client" form opens.
3. On the "Personal Information" tab fill in:
   - First Name * and Last Name * (required)
   - Email * (required; it is also the client's login name for the client portal)
   - Alias, Phone, Date of Birth, Status (Active, Inactive or Discharged), Referral Source, Boostapp ID
   - Therapist: click the field and pick the client's therapist from the list
   - Protocol: click the field and pick a protocol template, if one applies
4. On the "Medical Information" tab you can add Diagnosis, Medical History and Emergency Contact.
5. Click Save. The client appears in the table, and the system creates a user account for the client automatically (a notification confirms it). Ask your administrator for the initial password to give to the client.

## Find, edit or open a client
- To find a client, type in the filter box under the Last Name (or another) column header.
- To edit: click Edit at the end of the client's row, change the fields and click Save.
- To see everything about a client, click the row. The details popup has tabs for the client's Workout Plan, Session Reports, Statistics, Home Feedback, Appointments and Details.

## Create a therapist
1. In the sidebar click Therapists.
2. Click Add.
3. Fill in First Name * and Last Name * (required), Email, Phone, Specialization and License Number. Keep Active checked for a therapist who is working.
4. Click Save. If an email was entered, a user account for the therapist portal is created automatically.

## Deactivate a therapist
Click Edit on the therapist's row, clear the Active checkbox and click Save. Clicking the therapist's row opens a popup with their details and a Clients tab listing their clients.
