# Physio by Layer 8 - Overview and navigation

Physio is a physiotherapy management system for clinic administrators and therapists.

## The sidebar (left side)
Every page is opened from the sidebar. Click an entry to open it:

- Dashboard - the head-therapist dashboard (overview of clients and activity)
- Therapists - the clinic's therapists
- Clients - the clients (patients)
- Exercises - the exercise library
- Protocol Templates - reusable exercise protocols
- Treatment Plans - the plan prescribed to a client
- Appointments - sessions scheduled between clients and therapists
- Progress Logs - pain level and notes logged per client
- Session Reports - reports of completed sessions
- Home Feedback - feedback clients report from their home exercises
- Boostapp Calendar - events imported from the Boostapp booking system (read-only)
- Workout Builder - builds a workout from a client's posture, joint and protocol
- System - administration: Health, Security (Users, Roles, Credentials, Events), Modules, Logs, Data Import

## Working with any table
Most pages are a table of records:
- Add: click the Add button above the table to open an empty form. Fields marked with * are required.
- Open details: click a row to see the record in a popup.
- Edit: click the Edit button at the end of the row, change the fields and click Save.
- Delete: click the Delete button at the end of the row and confirm in the "Confirm Delete" popup.
- Filter: type in the filter box under a column header to narrow the rows to that column's value.
- Sort: click a column header to sort by it.
- Paging: use the pagination bar under the table to change the page or the page size.
- Export: open the export menu above the table and choose CSV, Excel or PDF.
- Forms with several sections show a tab per section (for example "Personal Information" and "Medical Information"); click a tab to reach its fields.
- Fields that point at another record (for example Therapist on a client) have a picker: click the field, search in the list, select the record and click Select.

## The AI assistant (chat bubble)
The round chat button at the bottom-right opens the AI assistant.
- Ask a question about your data normally, for example "how many active clients do I have".
- Start the question with @help to get guidance on the menus and how to do something, for example "@help how do I create a client".
- Start the question with @web for a general question that has nothing to do with this system, for example "@web what is a good warm-up before running". Questions about this system's own data are not accepted with @web.
- Click New in the chat panel to start a new conversation.
